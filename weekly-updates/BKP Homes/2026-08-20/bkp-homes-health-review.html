F3 Verify Active now 240 warnings 30d baseline empty

Voice minutes are not going through the credit ledger this tenant emits. Settlement says “No reservation found, nothing to settle” once per inbound (240), and credit_settled is zero for 30 days while other tenants settle thousands a day.

This is not “credit is leaking.” Nothing was reserved for the call in the first place (reserves this week are only WhatsApp 9.95 and webhook 1.05 estimated credits). Reconcile against the billing ledger before anyone mentions money on a call.

Fix. MagickVoice platform/billing: confirm whether inbound Gemini Live is supposed to reserve+settle, and whether BKP Homes is billed on a different path. Do not open a customer credit conversation until the ledger agrees.

Loki MagickVoice-Platform component=settlement-service warn n=240 msg="No reservation found, nothing to settle" · credit_settled this_tenant=0 every day in 30d baseline; all_tenants up to 8,519/day

F4 Friction Active now 89 / 209 resolved 172 neutral

The agent takes every call and closes fewer than half of them, by its own scoring. The bulk is neutral with a 40 percent resolution rate inside that bulk — the standard polite-but-not-closing shape.

They already rewrote the prompt on 14 Aug (9 prompt_updated). Resolution is model-scored, not a customer-confirmed outcome.

F5 Friction Active now 240 / 240 fallback 23 never reached voice

09 · Method

What these numbers can and cannot say

  • First run. No prior MEMORIES.md. Account age (75 days) is from groups.created_at, not a range scan. Timeline history starts this week.
  • Windows. PostHog events and Loki logs both cover 2026-08-13 07:41 UTC to 2026-08-20 07:41 UTC (7 days). 20 Aug is a partial UTC day. Credit_settled baseline is 30 days and is labelled as baseline, not customer volume.
  • Sources. PostHog project 454290, filter $group_0 = b741952f…. Loki {deployment_environment="production", service_name=~"MagickVoice.*"} | tenantId= that UUID. Short token bkp.
  • Prometheus. Instant count by (__name__) ({tenant=~".+"}) on grafanacloud-prom returned empty. No metric carries a tenant label. Checked once.
  • Governed catalog consulted: no match for a named tenant health metric; all figures below are noncanonical event/log aggregations.
  • Resolution and sentiment are model-scored from call_analyzed, not customer-confirmed outcomes.
  • Connect rate uses properties.connected; success rate would use status. Here they agree (217/217).
  • Gemini Live cost is unpriced in PostHog, not zero.
  • matched_intent on call_completed is not in the event schema (always null). Intent claims use inbound_call_resolved only.
  • Denominators under 10 are labelled: TRANSFER_FAILED 3/40, negative sentiment 0/1, prompt 400 n=1.
  • Cannot tell: whether voice minutes are billed on a path we do not emit; whether /super-admin/usage dead clicks are staff; the HTTP status of the 6 webhook failures (no Loki error line); anything before 13 Aug except created_at and the 30d credit baseline.
