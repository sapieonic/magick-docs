What these numbers can and cannot say

  • Third weekly run. Identity, F-numbers, and history before 22 Aug come from MEMORIES.md. Account age (84 days) is from groups.created_at, not a range scan. Two new Default account keys (3d4ab1ca, b078da10) were appended from $group_1; they emitted only identify events.
  • Windows. PostHog events and Loki logs both cover 2026-08-22 06:37 UTC to 2026-08-29 06:37 UTC (7 days). Both sources cover the same seven days. 29 Aug is a partial UTC day. Credit_settled baseline is 30 days and is labelled as baseline, not customer volume.
  • Sources. PostHog project 454290, filter $group_0 = b741952f-e815-430b-8136-8b4b59070a3e. Loki {deployment_environment="production", service_name=~"MagickVoice.*"} | tenantId= that UUID. Short token bkp.
  • Prometheus. Instant count by (__name__) ({tenant=~".+"}) on grafanacloud-prom returned empty. No metric carries a tenant label. Checked once.
  • Governed catalog consulted: no match for a named tenant health metric; all figures below are noncanonical event/log aggregations.
  • Resolution and sentiment are model-scored from call_analyzed, not customer-confirmed outcomes.
  • Connect rate uses properties.connected (185/185). Success rate uses status (183/185 completed-or-escalated vs 2 failed). They disagree this week.
  • Gemini Live cost is unpriced in PostHog, not zero.
  • matched_intent on call_completed is in the schema but unused for intent claims; inbound_call_resolved is the source (200/200 false).
  • Denominators under 10 are labelled: TRANSFER_FAILED 2/34, webhook failed 1/106, pipeline error 2/185, roster unanswered 3/14, mixed resolved 7/19, WhatsApp fails 6.
  • F10 sequence vs cause. Failures stopped after they relinked the QR. "Relinking fixed them" is an inference — labelled as one.
  • Cannot tell: whether voice minutes are billed on a path we do not emit; the HTTP status of the 1 webhook failure (no Loki error line); the body of the 3 call-session warns; whether the preset number is wrong or the carrier is rejecting it; anything before 22 Aug except the ledger and the 30d credit baseline.
