New account Sreenidhi_vijayawada (db5c9cfe, created 2026-08-29) was appended from a property read on account_id; it emitted identify plus campaign_composer_opened only.
  • Windows. PostHog events and Loki logs both cover 2026-08-29 06:30 UTC to 2026-09-05 06:30 UTC (7 days). Both sources cover the same seven days. 29 Aug and 5 Sep are partial UTC days. Credit_settled baseline is 30 days and is labelled as baseline, not customer volume.
  • Sources. PostHog project 454290, filter $group_0 = b741952f-e815-430b-8136-8b4b59070a3e. Loki {deployment_environment="production", service_name=~"MagickVoice.*"} | tenantId= that UUID. Short token bkp. Section 4 name resolution was skipped; stored IDs returned data.
  • Prometheus. Instant count by (__name__) ({tenant=~".+"}) on grafanacloud-prom returned empty. No metric carries a tenant label. Checked once.
  • Governed catalog consulted: no match for a named tenant health metric; all figures below are noncanonical event/log aggregations.
  • Resolution and sentiment are model-scored from call_analyzed, not customer-confirmed outcomes.
  • Connect rate uses properties.connected (230/230). Success rate uses status (229/230 not failed vs 1 failed). They disagree this week.
  • Gemini Live cost is unpriced in PostHog, not zero.
  • matched_intent on call_completed is in the schema but unused for intent claims; inbound_call_resolved is the source (253/253 false).
  • Denominators under 10 are labelled: TRANSFER_FAILED 3/41, webhook failed 2/97, pipeline error 1/230, mixed resolved 6/16, 300 s timeouts n=2, dead clicks n=2, api_error n=4.
  • F6 and F10 stay on the ledger as resolved. F6 no longer appears in this week’s data; F10 held at zero WhatsApp fails.
  • Cannot tell: whether voice minutes are billed on a path we do not emit; the HTTP status of the 2 webhook failures (no Loki error line); the body of the 2 call-session warns; whether the preset number is wrong or the carrier is rejecting it; anything before 29 Aug except the ledger and the 30d credit baseline.
