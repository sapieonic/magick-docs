  • Governed catalog consulted: no match for a named tenant health metric; all figures below are noncanonical event/log aggregations.
  • Resolution and sentiment are model-scored from call_analyzed, not customer-confirmed outcomes.
  • Connect rate uses properties.connected (230/230). Success rate uses status (229/230 not failed vs 1 failed). They disagree this week.
  • Gemini Live cost is unpriced in PostHog, not zero.
  • matched_intent on call_completed is in the schema but unused for intent claims; inbound_call_resolved is the source (250/250 false).
  • Denominators under 10 are labelled: TRANSFER_FAILED 3/47, webhook failed 6/91, pipeline error 1/230, mixed resolved 6/15, 300 s timeout n=1, dead clicks n=2, console users n=1, 11 Sep resolved 3/25.
