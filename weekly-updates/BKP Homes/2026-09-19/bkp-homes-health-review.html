Credit_settled baseline is 30 days and is labelled as baseline, not customer volume. Section 5.10’s two windows collapsed to the same 7-day list.
  • Sources. PostHog project 454290, filter $group_0 = b741952f-e815-430b-8136-8b4b59070a3e. Loki {deployment_environment="production", service_name=~"MagickVoice.*"} | tenantId= that UUID. Short token bkp.
  • Prometheus. Instant count by (__name__) ({tenant=~".+"}) on grafanacloud-prom returned empty. No metric carries a tenant label. Checked once.
  • Governed catalog consulted: no match for a named tenant health metric; all figures are noncanonical event/log aggregations.
  • Volume proxy. After 13 Sep 05:38, unique properties.call_id on $ai_generation where model is Gemini Live, plus Loki call-session / ivr-engine counts, stand in for call_completed. Last week those unique IDs (224) matched completed calls (230) within 3%. This week they do not (221 vs 18), and the same completed-call stop is platform-wide.
  • Resolution and sentiment are model-scored from call_analyzed, not customer-confirmed outcomes. n=15 only.
  • Connect rate uses properties.connected on the 18 completed rows (18/18). Success rate uses status (18/18 not failed). After the cutoff both are unknown.
  • Gemini Live cost is unpriced in PostHog, not zero. gpt-4o analysis is $3.94.
  • Denominators under 10 are labelled: transfers 0/4, webhook failed 1/7, mixed sentiment n=1, positive n=2, pdf-ingest error n=1, call-session warn n=1, KB 403 n=2, /proxy/calls 404 n=2.
  • F6 and F10 stay on the ledger as resolved 2026-08-25. F12 resolved this run after 306 clean call-list loads. F8 resolved this run (0 dead clicks).
  • Cannot tell: transfer outcomes after 13 Sep 04:46; webhook HTTP status of the 1 fail (no Loki error line); BYOC 105 x 404 mechanism; whether voice minutes are billed on a path we do not emit; unique console seat count (pageviews absent); anything before 12 Sep except the ledger and the 30d credit baseline.
