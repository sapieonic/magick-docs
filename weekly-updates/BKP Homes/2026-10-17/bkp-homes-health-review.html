09 · Method

What these numbers can and cannot say

  • Tenth weekly run. The ledger already had this tenant, so the name was not re-resolved. Call sessions are still only account cc0bd2e4-388e-4512-bb5e-1a0b41996f81. No new account id appeared.
  • Both sources cover the same seven days: 2026-10-10 06:30:00 UTC inclusive through 2026-10-17 06:30:00 UTC exclusive. PostHog queries use that bound on timestamp. Loki instant queries use a range ending at 2026-10-17T06:30:00Z. Days in the charts are [1d] instants ending at each 06:30 boundary, so a midnight bucket does not borrow the previous morning. The seven session buckets sum to 224, which matches the 7-day total.
  • One longer read, labeled as a baseline and not as this customer’s week: credit_settled from 17 Sep 06:30 through 17 Oct 06:30, this tenant against all tenants. The event only exists on 2-9 Oct in that span.
  • The prior-week column is the 10 Oct ledger (3 Oct 06:30 through 10 Oct 06:30). It was not re-queried. History older than this window comes from that ledger.
  • Prometheus count by (__name__) ({tenant=~".+"}) on grafanacloud-prom at 2026-10-17T06:30:00Z returned no series. No metric carries a tenant label. Checked once.
  • The governed metric catalog for this project is empty (0 metrics). Every figure in this report is a one-off query, not a saved definition.
  • Resolution and sentiment are model grades of the transcript. They were not produced this week. The 98 of 203 figure is the 9 Oct score, kept so the finding does not vanish. Gemini cost is unpriced. Do not read it as zero.
  • A zero completion count is missing instrumentation. Every tenant is at zero, while this tenant’s server logs continued at a normal daily rate. It is not a silent desk.
  • Loki raw lines were not used. Counts are aggregations on structured metadata. The settlement warning text and the call-session warning text did not group. The Gemini 1011 reason grouped and is quoted from that label; the label itself is truncated after “billi”.
  • Account age uses groups.created_at 2026-06-06 13:03 UTC, which is 133 days at this cutoff.
  • Other tenants’ ids on the shared 1011 are not listed. The count is 6 tenants and 20 closes.
