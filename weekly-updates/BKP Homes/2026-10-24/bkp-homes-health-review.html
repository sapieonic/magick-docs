  • Prometheus was checked once: count by (__name__) ({tenant=~".+"}) on grafanacloud-prom at 2026-10-24 06:30 UTC returned no series. No metric carries a tenant label. Latency and errors above are Loki, not Prometheus.
  • There is no governed metric in the catalog for these counts. Every figure in this report is a one-off calculation for this week, not a saved definition.
  • Eleventh weekly run. The ledger already held identity, findings F1-F20, and the timeline. F21 and F22 are new. No finding was renumbered. Held-closed items were checked and left closed.
  • Absent is not declined. Zero pageviews, zero automation events, and zero completions mean the event was not emitted. They do not mean the customer stopped, and they do not mean a feature is gated. HTTP 403 was 0. The branching gate was not remeasured.
