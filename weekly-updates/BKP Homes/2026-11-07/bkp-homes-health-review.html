  • Thirteenth weekly run. The ledger already had this tenant, so the name was not looked up again. Call-session accountId is only Inbound, 191 of 191. No new account id.
  • Both sources cover the same seven days: 2026-10-31 06:30:00 UTC through 2026-11-07 06:30:00 UTC. PostHog events and Loki logs.   • Pageview dates in section 07 are calendar dates in UTC. They are a different cut from the service day, which is why they are not on the call chart.
  • One longer read, labeled as a baseline and not as this customer's week: credit_settled from 8 Oct 06:30 through 7 Nov 06:30. Only 8 Oct and 9 Oct have rows.
  • The prior-week column is the ledger for 24 Oct 06:30 through 31 Oct 06:30. It was not re-queried. Product-event zeros in that column are a dark pipe.
  • Prometheus count by (__name__) ({tenant=~".+"}) on grafanacloud-prom returned no series at 2026-11-07 06:30 UTC. No metric carries a tenant label. Checked once.
  • The governed metric catalog for this project is empty (0 metrics). Every figure in this report is a one-off query, not a saved definition.
  • Resolution and sentiment were not remeasured. The 9 Oct grade is a model reading its own transcript. Do not brief it as this week. Gemini cost is unpriced because generations are not being captured. Do not read that as zero dollars.
  • A zero on call_completed is missing instrumentation. The same names are absent for every tenant, while server sessions continued and console events resumed. It is not a silent desk.
  • Loki raw lines were not used. Counts are aggregations on structured metadata. The settlement warning text did not group, so it is not quoted as freshly read. Greenapi errors grouped only on status=400.

  • Account age uses groups.created_at 2026-06-06 13:03 UTC, which is 154 days at this cutoff.
