09 · Method

What these numbers are, and what they are not

  • Both sources cover the same seven days: 2026-11-07 06:30:00 UTC inclusive through 2026-11-14 06:30:00 UTC exclusive. Loki retention is inside that window. There is no "logs are shorter than events" gap this week.
  • PostHog project 454290, tenant $group_0 = b741952f-e815-430b-8136-8b4b59070a3e. Loki datasource grafanacloud-logs, stream labels only service_name and deployment_environment=production. Prometheus datasource grafanacloud-prom, count by (__name__) ({tenant=~".+"}) at the window end, returned empty. No metric carries a tenant label. One check.
  • Service days are 06:30 to 06:30, labeled by the start day. Seven instant [1d] queries sum to the 7-day totals: sessions 561, analysis 487, IVR 545, quality 556, WhatsApp 473, settlement warns 597, Gemini info 39,090, Gemini errors 5. A midnight-aligned range is not used.
  • Page views are UTC calendar dates. They are not plotted on the session chart.
  • The 30-day baseline is only for credit_settled, 15 Oct 06:30 through 14 Nov 06:30. It is a baseline, not this customer's usage. Result: 0 events, 0 tenants.
  • No governed metric in the catalog matches these counts. Every figure is a one-off for this week, not a saved definition.
  • Account age is groups.created_at for the tenant, 2026-06-06, which is 161 days at this cutoff. It is not a range scan. Identity was reused from the ledger. Call sessions this week are all account cc0bd2e4, 561 of 561. No new account id.
  • Connect rate, direction, talk time, sentiment, resolution, carrier on the session, model name, automation send success, and credit amounts are absent from this window. Absence of the call events is platform-wide, so it is not this account going quiet. Absence of a feature they never opened would be a different sentence. Branching is the opposite: they opened it 46 times.
  • Model-scored resolution from October is not restated as this week. Settlement warning text did not group, so no body is quoted. workflowId did not group, including on a one-day window, so 5,124 dispatcher lines are not split by rule and are not send receipts.
  • Seven page views on other workspaces are omitted, and so are the ids on those paths. Four super-admin dead clicks are counted and not listed. The Gemini provider text is described, not linked.
  • History older than these seven days is the ledger, not a wider query. This is the fourteenth weekly run.
