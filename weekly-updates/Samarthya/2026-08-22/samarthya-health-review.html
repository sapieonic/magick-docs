09 · Method

What these numbers can and cannot say

  • Third weekly run. Identity and F-numbers come from MEMORIES.md. Account age (67 days) is from groups.created_at, not a range scan. Timeline before 15 Aug is ledger-only.
  • Windows. PostHog events and Loki logs both cover 2026-08-15 04:30 UTC to 2026-08-22 04:30 UTC (7 days). Both sources cover the same seven days. 22 Aug is a partial UTC Saturday morning and has no dials; counts froze around 04:30. Credit_settled baseline is 30 days and is labelled as baseline, not this week’s customer data.
  • Sources. PostHog project 454290, filter $group_0 = ee122920-8af6-4c0d-af1a-3bf4d93c1414. Loki {deployment_environment=~"production|dedicated|prod", service_name=~"MagickVoice.*"} | tenantId= that UUID. Short token samarthya. Service that carries HTTP: MagickVoice-Platform-Dedicated. Voice path logs: MagickVoice-Orchestrator-Dedicated (tenantId present).
  • Prometheus. Instant count by (__name__) ({tenant=~".+"}) on grafanacloud-prom returned empty. No metric carries a tenant label. Checked once.
  • Governed catalog consulted: no match for a named tenant health metric; all figures are noncanonical event/log aggregations.
  • Resolution and sentiment are model-scored from call_analyzed, not customer-confirmed outcomes.
  • Connect rate uses properties.connected; status success uses status. They disagree here because voicemail is connected and not status=completed.
  • Gemini Live cost is unpriced in PostHog, not zero. gpt-4o analysis is $46.59.
  • call_completed does not stamp $group_1. Per-account voice volume is Loki HTTP (Meesho BOT dominates), not PostHog calls.
  • matched_intent on call_completed is always null. Do not read 0 matched intents as a prompt miss.
  • Denominators under 10 are labelled: inbound n=3, campaign_launch_failed n=1, Platform error-level n=3, 502 n=2, last-48h 429 n=1.
  • Cannot tell: which Gemini Live model the 41 pipeline errors used (generation events carry no error flag); whether the 19 Aug not-connected spike was a VoBiz code or a list; whether F10 polling was fixed or merely quiet after 20 Aug; anything before 15 Aug except created_at, the ledger, and the 30d credit baseline.
