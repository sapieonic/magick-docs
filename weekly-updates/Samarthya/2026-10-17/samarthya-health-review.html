09 · Method

What these numbers are, and what they are not.

  • Sources: PostHog project 454290, timezone UTC, and Grafana Loki grafanacloud-logs. Both cover the same seven days: 2026-10-10 04:32 UTC through 2026-10-17 04:38 UTC. The instant Loki range is a trailing [7d] evaluated at 04:32-04:38 on 17 Oct.
  • This is the eleventh run. Identity was reused from the ledger: tenant ee122920-8af6-4c0d-af1a-3bf4d93c1414, created 2026-06-16, 123 days. The account set under it did not grow. Events still arrive on that tenant id, so the name was not re-resolved.
  • The daily bars are calendar days 10-16 Oct, from Loki [1d] buckets ending at midnight UTC. The 10 Oct bar includes 00:00-04:30, which is before the product window. Saturday 17 Oct 00:00-04:38 is inside the week totals and is not a bar.
  • The 30-day credit_settled query is a baseline, not this customer’s week. This tenant settled every day from 3 Oct through 9 Oct. No tenant settled from 10 Oct through 17 Oct. That is why a settlement gap is not briefed as a leak.
  • Prometheus count by (__name__) ({tenant=~”.+”}) on grafanacloud-prom returned no series. Checked once. No metric here carries a tenant label.
  • No governed catalog metric matched this review. Every figure is a one-off query for this account and this week.
  • Log lines are not calls. Gemini Live info is 515,251. Call session is about 76,000 and its message label is empty. The analysis dispatcher is 17,987, message label empty. The settlement dispatcher is 110,125, message label empty. None of those is a volume to quote.
  • Model-scored sentiment was not available. Last week’s resolution rate is not repeated as this week’s result. Gemini Live cost is unpriced, not zero.
  • $exception is the check that browser errors would have been seen: 74 on other tenants, 0 here. A dark $pageview is a different fact, because that event is zero everywhere.
