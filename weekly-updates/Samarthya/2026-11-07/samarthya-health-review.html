09 · Method

What these numbers are, and what they are not.

  • Sources. PostHog project 454290, events filtered on $group_0 = ee122920-8af6-4c0d-af1a-3bf4d93c1414. Loki datasource grafanacloud-logs, services MagickVoice-Orchestrator-Dedicated and MagickVoice-Platform-Dedicated, deployment_environment production, dedicated, or prod, pipeline filter tenantId. Prometheus datasource grafanacloud-prom.
  • Window, both sources, the same seven days. Loki instant queries use [7d] ending 2026-11-07T04:45:00Z, so the start is 2026-10-31T04:45:00Z. PostHog uses timestamp >= now() minus 7 days, evaluated the same morning. This tenant’s first product event in the window is 2026-11-02 04:32:56 UTC. The last dead click is 2026-11-07 03:20:28 UTC. Saturday 31 Oct and Sunday 1 Nov are inside the window and are zeros, not missing days. Loki retention covers this range. There is no gap where logs are shorter than events.
  • Daily buckets. A Loki count_over_time bucket timestamp is the end of the window. The point at 2026-11-05 00:00 UTC is Wednesday 4 Nov. The bucket at 2026-10-31 00:00 UTC is Friday 30 Oct and was dropped; its Gemini count of 1,463 matches last week’s Friday tail. Gemini, webhook, settlement, VoiceLink, and HTTP 404 midnight bars for the in-window days match the 7-day totals exactly. HTTP 200 midnight bars sum to 14,341 against a 7-day total of 14,319. The 22-line difference is the slice of Saturday 31 Oct before 04:45.
  • Baseline, not this week. credit_settled over 30 days has two rows, 8 Oct and 9 Oct. On 9 Oct this tenant is 3,150 of 10,730 across 4 tenants. Nothing after 9 Oct, for anyone. That is why a settlement gap is an instrumentation question.
  • Prometheus. count by (__name__) of {tenant=~".+"} returned no series. No metric carries a tenant label. Checked once, 2026-11-07. Not used.
  • Lines are not calls. Gemini, static, webhook, settlement, and analysis-dispatcher counts are log lines. Code 1000 closes are lines. Campaign recipient counts are the one product number that is safe to brief as volume, and they are who was queued, not who connected.
  • Catalog. The governed metric catalog has no approved call, credit, or delivery definition. Every figure in this report is a one-off query for this week, not a saved metric.
  • Identity. Tenant and account ids were taken from the running ledger and confirmed by this week’s events and logs. Display names that changed: 05ff00e4 is now (DPD 1) Meesho Bot_October_NIYOGIN/PAYU, and c8122ab2 is now (DPD 1) Meesho Bot_October_SNAPMINT. New group rows this week: e68b6f80 (DPD 4) Meesho_Niyogin/PayU on 2 Nov, 1bdfb8fd (DPD 4) Meesho_Snapmint on 4 Nov, and c5972f1d Default on 2 Nov. Group created_at on the October accounts is the day PostHog first recorded the group, not the day the account was born. Those accounts already had voice last week.
  • What this cannot tell you. Connect rate, talk time, sentiment, resolution, model cost, and whether a webhook was accepted. Sentiment and resolution, when they exist, are a model grading its own transcript. Absence of an automation event is not proof the customer turned the feature off. HTTP 403 was 0, so the dark automation events are not a feature gate we can see.
