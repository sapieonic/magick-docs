09 · Method

What was counted, and what was not.

  • Tenant ee122920-8af6-4c0d-af1a-3bf4d93c1414, display name Samarthya's Organization, created 2026-06-16 06:11 UTC, 158 days at this run. The stored id returned events, starting 2026-11-14 05:05 UTC, so the name scan was skipped. Short Loki token remains samarthya. It was not used, because Loki was not queried.
  • PostHog window, pinned: now() at query time was 2026-11-21 04:35:34 UTC, so the SQL bound is 2026-11-14 04:35:34 UTC through 2026-11-21 04:35:34 UTC. Project 454290, timezone UTC. $group_0 is the tenant, $group_1 is the account.
  • Loki was not queried. Grafana Cloud asked for an interactive sign-in that this run cannot complete. The Prometheus check count by (__name__) ({tenant=~".+"}) was not run. Last week's 2xx rate, latency, Gemini close codes, static line counts, and webhook line counts stay in the ledger with their dates. They are not this week's measurements.
  • PostHog's own log table has 25 rows in the same seven days, all posthog-browser-logs, with no tenant attribute. They were not assigned to this customer. Three of them name staging agency hosts and are out of scope.
  • credit_settled over 30 days, the baseline and not a customer total: zero rows for every tenant. That is an instrumentation gap, not a leak. The ledger's last settled days are 8-9 Oct, which this 30-day window no longer reaches.
  • $exception is 0 here and 66 on 6 other tenants, so a silent exception pipe is not the explanation for an empty call tape. The call events are absent everywhere, while pageviews and api_error are present on many tenants.
  • Days with no events are absent rows. The chart pads 15 Nov and 21 Nov to zero. 21 Nov is a partial day, through 04:35 UTC. 14 Nov starts at 04:35 UTC. The 2,382-person launch at 05:09 is inside this window and was not in last week's report.
  • There is no governed metric for these counts. They are one-off queries. Sentiment and resolution, when they exist, are model-scored. They do not exist this week.
  • What these numbers cannot tell you: whether any of the 19,207 people were dialed, connected, or graded; whether a webhook fired; whether Gemini closed cleanly; whether the IVR path logged an error. The customer retried both failed uploads. That part is observed. It is not a claim that the retry changed the platform.
