09 · Method

What these numbers can and cannot say

  • First weekly run. MEMORIES.md did not exist. Identity, findings, and timeline are created at the end of this run. Account age (7 days) is from groups.created_at (2026-08-30T03:02:31Z), not a range scan.
  • Windows. PostHog events and Loki logs both cover 2026-08-30 15:23:36 UTC to 2026-09-06 15:23:36 UTC (7 days). 30 Aug and 6 Sep are partial UTC days. The credit_settled baseline is 30 days and is labelled baseline, not this week’s customer data. 5.11 uses a 21-day scan and is quoted only in §07. 5.12 / 5.13 use the 10-day windows written in the prompt; LLM figures in §03 are the 7-day re-run (734 generations) so they match the inventory.
  • Sources. PostHog project 454290, filter $group_0 = 474a3882-b176-4135-95a6-059d7c1061f7. Short token sreenidhi. Loki datasource grafanacloud-logs, deployment_environment=production, service MagickVoice-Platform-Dedicated. Live account Default 2561527b-9386-42c2-b579-ada5e292bced. Sreenidhi_vijayawada db5c9cfe has no 7-day $group_1 events. Four other $group_1 keys appear only as $groupidentify (shared-browser residue) and are not used for volume.
  • Prometheus. Instant count by (__name__) ({tenant=~".+"}) on grafanacloud-prom returned empty. No metric carries a tenant label. Recorded.
  • Governed catalog consulted: no match (metric-list count=0). All figures are noncanonical event aggregations.
  • Resolution and sentiment are model-scored from call_analyzed, not customer-confirmed outcomes.
  • Connect rate uses properties.connected; status success uses status. They disagree because 279 voicemails are connected and not status=completed.
  • Gemini Live cost is unpriced in PostHog, not zero. gpt-4o analysis is $2.32 this window.
  • matched_intent on call_completed is null on every row. Do not read 0 as a prompt miss.
  • call_completed often does not stamp $group_1. Per-account voice volume is inferred from campaign and Loki HTTP (Default).
  • No Orchestrator lines matched tenantId this window. Dedicated platform logs cover the same seven days as events; they do not include the realtime voice path.
  • Denominators under 10 are labelled: IVR sessions n=4, Telugu completed n=3, positive scored n=4, negative scored n=5, audio 500 n=2 (PostHog) / n=4 (Loki), prompt 400/404 n=1+1, rage clicks n=2, seats n=2.
  • Credits. reserved 63594 = released 32538 + settled 31054 (off by 2). 30-day baseline: this tenant dominates project-wide credit_settled. Reconcile against the ledger; not a leak.
  • Cannot tell: why they left AI voice (connect vs opener vs cost) — sequence only; what the 15,622 unbucketed static rows are; whether Default is supposed to have BYOC DIDs; anything before 30 Aug 15:23 except created_at, 5.11, and the 30d credit baseline; Orchestrator / Prometheus tenant health.
