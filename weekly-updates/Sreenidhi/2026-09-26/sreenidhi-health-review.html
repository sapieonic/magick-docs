09 · Method

  • Windows. PostHog and Loki both cover 2026-09-19 05:40 UTC to 2026-09-26 05:40 UTC. Same seven days. 19 Sep and 26 Sep are partial. Daily bars use Loki count_over_time[1d] buckets, which are stamped at the end of the window: the bar labelled Sat 19 is the bucket ending Sun 20 00:00 UTC. 23 and 24 Sep are absent rows, padded as zeros. The credit and event-liveness checks are a 30-day baseline, labelled as baseline, not as this week’s customer usage. The prior-7d column is 12 Sep 05:40 to 19 Sep 05:40 and is comparison only.
  • Sources. PostHog project 454290, timezone UTC, filter $group_0 = 474a3882-b176-4135-95a6-059d7c1061f7. Identity reused from the ledger after the 7-day inventory came back empty, then confirmed: the groups row is unchanged (sreenidhi, created 2026-08-30T03:02:31Z, 27 days). Accounts unchanged: Default 2561527b-9386-42c2-b579-ada5e292bced and Sreenidhi_vijayawada db5c9cfe-5ad5-4836-98c4-c84a111a84d6. The quiet account still has no $group_1 volume and no Loki accountId. Loki datasource grafanacloud-logs. Platform = deployment_environment=production, service MagickVoice-Platform-Dedicated. Orchestrator = deployment_environment=dedicated, service MagickVoice-Orchestrator-Dedicated.
  • Prometheus. Instant count by (__name__) ({tenant=~".+"}) on grafanacloud-prom returned empty. No metric carries a tenant label. Recorded once.
  • Coverage that came back empty, on purpose. Call outcomes, direction, pipeline tier, talk time, sentiment, automations by id, messages by channel, credit reserve/release/settle, PostHog API errors, pageviews, dead clicks, rage clicks, LLM cost. Each is a zero event count for this tenant in the 7-day inventory, not a skipped query.
  • Instrumentation versus failure. 7-day all-tenant inventory: $ai_generation 32,978 / 17 tenants; $exception 28 / 6; messages_dispatched 2 / 1; call_analyzed 1 / 1; $groupidentify 2 / 1. No call_completed, $pageview, api_error, or credit_settled in that list. 30-day last-seen: those four stop on 13 Sep between 05:32 and 05:41 UTC. This tenant was 84,154 of 96,961 credit settlements and 5,440 of 51,077 completed calls in that 30 days, so the events were not historically sparse here. After 13 Sep they are zero for everyone. Do not read this week’s product-event zero as a Sreenidhi-only outage. Do read the AI-generation zero as theirs.
  • Credits. No reserve, release, or settle events this week. 30-day baseline last non-zero day is 13 Sep (this tenant 2,572 / all tenants 2,575). The Sunday gap stays “reconcile the ledger,” not “credit is leaking.”
  • Denominators under 10. HTTP 502 n=4 lines (2 access + 2 error-mask). HTTP 404 n=3. BYOC warns n=8. Last-24h lines n=5. Job 200s n=1 and n=3. Notification-deliver n=1, no message. None of these are rates.
  • Whose requests. Status lines are grouped by originator before anyone is blamed. Both UI originators land on account Default. The analytics originator is 35 lines and is not treated as a user.
  • Cannot tell. Which person opened the console. Whether the two 502 pairs are two attempts or four. Whether Sunday’s reservations are still held. Whether the 4,123 rows never dequeued are still queued. Why they stopped at 05:41 on 13 Sep. What the 828 orchestrator lines did, beyond “http, no URL, same days as the console.” Anything before 19 Sep 05:40 except created_at, the ledger, the prior-7d comparison, and the 30-day baseline.
